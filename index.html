PHARM-E-CO
棄「藥」投明

結合 AI 智能辨識與活性碳技術，重新定義廢棄藥物回收標準。 Redefining pharmaceutical waste recycling standards with AI recognition and activated carbon technology.

水污染，比你想得更近。
Water pollution is closer than you think.

香港城市大學研究顯示，啟德河檢測出高達 34 種藥物殘留，居全球研究河道之冠。這是我們必須面對的生態危機。
Research by City University of Hong Kong shows up to 34 types of pharmaceutical residues in Kai Tak River, ranking highest globally. This is an ecological crisis we must face.

環境危機的真相
The Truth of Environmental Crisis

  • 啟德河中抗生素濃度，超出環境安全標準高達 1.3 至 5.5 倍。Antibiotic concentrations in Kai Tak River exceed environmental safety standards by 1.3 to 5.5 times.
  • 本港水體中的藥物殘留高達 85% 為止痛藥。Up to 85% of pharmaceutical residues in local waters are painkillers.
  • 過期或未服完的藥物，往往被習慣性丟棄或直接沖入馬桶。Expired or unfinished medications are often habitually discarded or flushed down the toilet.

生態與健康的連鎖反應
Chain Reaction on Ecology & Health

  • 隨意棄置藥物會誘導細菌產生基因突變，衍生出「無藥可醫」的超級細菌。Improper disposal induces genetic mutations in bacteria, spawning untreatable "superbugs."
  • 化學藥物沿著食物鏈累積，最終危害人體健康。Chemical drugs accumulate along the food chain, ultimately harming human health.
  • 一般污水處理廠無法完全去除高毒性藥物成分，必須從源頭攔截。General sewage plants cannot fully remove highly toxic pharmaceutical components.

顛覆傳統，觸手可及。
Revolutionizing Tradition, Right at Your Fingertips.

告別傳統線下藥房（如萬寧、屈臣氏）繁瑣的人工分揀流程。我們的智能設備將直接進駐各個社區的樓下，為您帶來前所未有的便利與精準。
Say goodbye to the tedious manual sorting processes of traditional offline pharmacies (like Mannings and Watsons). Our smart devices will be placed directly downstairs in your community, bringing unprecedented convenience and precision.

傳統回收模式
Traditional Recycling Method

  • 需親自前往指定藥房門店，路途不便。Requires traveling to designated pharmacy stores, making it inconvenient.
  • 極度依賴店員進行人工分揀，過程繁瑣且耗時。Heavily relies on manual sorting by staff, which is tedious and time-consuming.
  • 受限於門店營業時間，無法隨時進行回收。Restricted by store operating hours, lacking 24/7 flexibility.

PHARM-E-CO 智能回收
PHARM-E-CO Smart Recycling

  • 設備會放置在各個社區的樓下，真正做到下樓即可回收。Devices are placed right downstairs in residential communities, offering ultimate accessibility.
  • 內置 AI 視覺識別，秒速完成自動分揀，大幅節省人工與時間。Built-in AI vision sorts drugs instantly, significantly saving manual labor and time.
  • 全自動化無人值守，精準度極高且全天候運作。Fully automated, unmanned, highly precise, and operates around the clock.

核心技術。強大，且環保。
Core Technology. Powerful and Eco-friendly.

這是一款專為屋苑設計的智能戶外裝置。我們捨棄一次性耗材，改用可重複使用的專用裝置，實現真正的永續發展。
Designed for residential estates, this smart outdoor device eliminates single-use consumables in favor of reusable components, achieving true sustainability.

AI 視覺神經網絡
AI Visual Neural Network

實時辨識藥物特徵。透過微型舵機滑道，精準地將藥物自動歸類投入「抗生素」、「止痛藥」或「其他藥物」收集箱，毫釐不差。
Real-time feature recognition. Using a micro-servo slide, it precisely categorizes drugs into "Antibiotics," "Painkillers," or "Other Drugs" collection bins with zero margin of error.

細顆粒活性碳技術
Fine Granular Activated Carbon

採用國際成熟方案 Deterra System 核心概念。結合機械對流攪拌，加速藥物吸附反應，極大幅度降低水溶液中的藥物殘留濃度。
Adopting the core concept of the mature Deterra System. Combined with mechanical convection stirring to accelerate adsorption, drastically reducing drug residue concentrations.

Combining AI intelligent classification with preliminary deactivation, the machine systematically completes all complex recycling processes behind the scenes.

雙滾筒粉碎 Twin-Roller Crushing

The crushing mechanism grinds pills to increase surface area, ensuring maximum reaction with activated carbon.

淨化原理，從分子層面瓦解危機。
Purification Principle: Neutralizing Threats at the Molecular Level.

我們的設備不僅僅是「收集」，更是直接在源頭進行「無害化處理」。透過物理粉碎與活性碳吸附，徹底阻斷藥物對環境的危害。
Our device goes beyond mere "collection" by performing "harmless treatment" directly at the source. Through physical crushing and activated carbon adsorption, it completely neutralizes environmental threats.

1. 破壞藥物屏障
Breaking the Barrier

藥錠外層通常包覆著糖衣或保護膜。機內的雙滾筒粉碎裝置能將藥物徹底碾碎，大幅增加接觸面積，為後續的吸附反應做好完美準備。
Pills often have protective coatings. The internal twin-roller crushing mechanism grinds them down, maximizing the surface area for the subsequent chemical reaction.

2. 巨量微孔吸附
Microporous Adsorption

特製的細顆粒活性碳內部佈滿納米級微孔。當粉碎後的藥物與水混合，活性成分會被強大的范德華力（Van der Waals force）牢牢牽引並鎖死在微孔中。
Specialized granular activated carbon is packed with nano-pores. When mixed with water, active pharmaceutical ingredients are tightly locked into these pores by Van der Waals forces.

3. 永久鈍化無害化
Permanent Deactivation

一旦藥物分子被活性碳結合，其化學結構的生物活性便遭到永久阻斷。此時的藥物殘渣已失去毒性，不會溶於水，也不會被生物吸收，徹底解除生態危機。
Once adsorbed, the biological activity of the drug molecules is permanently neutralized. The residue becomes non-toxic, insoluble, and safe, completely resolving the ecological crisis.
